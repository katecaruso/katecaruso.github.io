This is my EME 6208 design and development ePortfolio.

Welcome to my ePortfolio! I'm Kate Caruso, and my professional experience includes project management, website management, and instructional design. I enjoy finding practical and creative ways to use technology to make information easier to understand and learning more engaging. Through this course, Designing Integrated Media Environments, I'm building on that experience while exploring new tools and techniques in image editing, web design, video production, animation, screencasting, and mobile application development. This portfolio showcases my projects, what I've learned along the way, and how I can apply these skills to my work.
