Same Coffee, Different Story

Module 6 · Project 8 · Runway Gen-4.5 Screencast

Watch the screencast on YouTube

Reflection

For this project, I explored Runway, an AI video platform that can generate short clips from a written prompt or a starting image. For my screencast, I used the same image of a coffee cup to create two five-second clips. I wanted one to feel like a cozy café ad and the other to feel like the opening of a thriller. In the café version, steam rose from the cup as sunlight spread across the counter. In the thriller, a shadow passed behind the cup as the camera moved closer.

What surprised me was how much those choices changed the way I interpreted the scene. I would use this comparison with my learners to show how lighting, framing, and camera movement can influence what an audience expects to happen. I’d play both clips before revealing the prompts and ask learners to identify a specific moment that shaped their interpretation. Then I’d have them revise one line of a prompt to create a different mood and compare the result with the original. Learners could apply that same process when planning a presentation, an ad, or a training video.

Runway made it possible to try two visual approaches without filming or setting up lighting. It also showed me how much review is involved. I asked for the camera to creep forward in the thriller clip, but it moved so close that part of the cup went out of frame. I’d revise the prompt to specify that the whole cup should stay visible throughout the shot. Generating more versions also takes credits, so I’d have learners decide what they want to test before they generate another clip.

I would use Runway for activities where learners can compare versions and explain their production choices. The discussion I’d want to hear is what they changed, what the audience actually sees, and what they would do differently on the next pass.
