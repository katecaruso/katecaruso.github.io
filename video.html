Video Production

I created two videos. One was created using traditional editing tools, and the other was AI-assisted, to compare how each approach shaped my presentation of the material.

Watching the two versions side by side shows how choices about visuals, pacing, and narration can change how an explanation comes across.

Production Process

For both traditional and AI-assisted development, thinking about my message, visual design, and manual editing required about the same level of effort. I struggle with video creation because I keep going until I think it's as close to perfect as I can get. It feels tedious, but surprisingly, I enjoy it.

The AI-assisted process was more straightforward. Once I generated the images and built in the transitions and effects, it went very quickly. It's difficult to judge how much of the process was faster because AI helped and how much was faster because I'd just completed many of the same tasks using the traditional approach. Although it took many tries to record the script myself, I preferred my own voiceover to that of “Lisa,” the Canva AI voice.

Challenges and Solutions

One challenge I encountered was a communication issue. My topic was the history of the television show Friends, and ChatGPT tried to create images that conflicted with its own guidelines regarding copyrighted material. We had a little back-and-forth before I could craft the wording needed to generate the images. Going through that process was helpful and will inform future projects.

What I Learned

I liked how quickly AI could storyboard and script my video. Although I could veto or change anything that didn't hit the mark, having ChatGPT develop a concept for me saved time. Not having to search for all the images also helped. The traditional approach mainly helped me refresh skills I'd used before.

Overall, if I were assigned another educational video project, I would go straight to AI to brainstorm the concept, script, and storyboard. I still enjoy the editing process, even though I tend to spend too much time trying to get everything just right. The biggest thing I learned from the AI-assisted approach was how important it is to communicate exactly what I want while understanding AI's limitations.
